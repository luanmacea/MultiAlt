# Accessibility

MultiAlt should be usable by everyone who plays Roblox with more than one account. This page says what the app does today, where it still falls short, and how to tell us about a barrier.

## What the app supports

- **Interface size:** Settings › General › *Interface size* — Automatic (shrinks on small screens) or a fixed 80%–110%, applied to the whole app.
- **Small screens:** every screen scrolls and no window goes off the screen, down to the app's minimum window (750×450).
- **Keyboard:** pages, tabs, menus and dialogs work with the keyboard; `Esc` closes the topmost dialog or menu; focused controls show a visible outline.
- **Screen readers:** the sidebar, buttons and dialogs have names and roles, and the current page is announced.
- **Themes:** several color themes, plus a theme editor to raise contrast or change fonts.
- **Languages:** English, Português and Español.
- **Hidden names:** account names and pictures can be masked on every screen, useful when streaming or sharing screenshots.

## Known limitations

- Some account states (online, in game, invalid) are shown mainly by colored dots; a text legend is at the bottom of the window.
- The Roblox game windows themselves are outside MultiAlt's control.
- Accessibility has not been tested with every screen reader; NVDA and Narrator are the most likely to work, others are untested.
- macOS support is partial.

## Report a barrier

Use **Send feedback › Report a problem** inside the app, or open an [issue](https://github.com/luanmacea/MultiAlt/issues/new/choose) and mention "accessibility". Say what you tried to do, what assistive technology you use (if any) and what happened.
